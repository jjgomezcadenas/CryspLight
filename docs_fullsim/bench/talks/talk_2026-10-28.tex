\section{Menu 1: pedestal noise}

\begin{frame}{Menu 1: data and method}
\begin{columns}
\column{0.5\textwidth}
\small
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
Runs & Purpose & HG shaping & HoldDelay \\
\midrule
91--93 & shaping & 25, 50, 87.5 ns & 0 \\
94--98 & window & 25 ns & 96--496 ns \\
99 & stability & 25 ns & 0 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\medskip
SiPMs at 36.5~V, periodic trigger at 1~kHz, 10\,000 events per board and run, preamplifier
gains 55, peak detectors in peak-stretch mode, low gain shaped at 87.5~ns.
\column{0.5\textwidth}
\begin{itemize}
\item Pedestal $p_i$: median of the channel over the run; deviation $d_i = v_i - p_i$.
\item Common mode of a chip in an event: mean of the $d_i$ of its 32 channels, read at the
  same trigger.
\item Each channel takes the common mode with a slope $b_i$: coherent noise $b_i\,\mathrm{CM}$,
  incoherent noise the remainder.
\item Jumps of the chip (common mode beyond six robust widths) and single-channel outliers
  (beyond five) are set aside.
\end{itemize}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}
