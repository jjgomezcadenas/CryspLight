\section{Dishes served}

\begin{itemize}
\item Every channel of the Citiroc stores, at each trigger, the maximum of its shaped signal
  inside a window (peak-stretch mode). With the SiPMs below breakdown there is no signal: the
  stored value is the pedestal of the channel, and its fluctuation is the noise of the
  electronics.
\item The peak detector is the stage of the Citiroc after the slow shaper. In peak-stretch
  mode it follows the shaped signal from the opening of the window, holds the highest value
  reached, and stops at the hold (HoldDelay after the trigger); that value is what the ADC
  reads.
\item Menu 1 measures, for every channel of every board:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item the pedestal and its noise;
  \item how the noise depends on the shaping time of the slow shaper and on the length of
    the window (HoldDelay);
  \item how much of the noise is common to the channels of a chip (coherent) and how much
    is proper to each channel (incoherent).
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\section{Method}

The method is that of the noise note~\cite{noisenote}. For channel $i$ of a chip and event $e$,
with $v_i(e)$ the high-gain value:
\begin{align*}
p_i &= \operatorname{median}_e v_i(e), & d_i(e) &= v_i(e) - p_i, &
\mathrm{CM}(e) &= \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i} d_i(e),\\
d_i(e) &= b_i\,\mathrm{CM}(e) + r_i(e), &
\sigma_\mathrm{coh}^2 &= \langle b_i^2\,\mathrm{CM}^2\rangle, &
\sigma_\mathrm{inc}^2 &= \langle r_i^2\rangle, \qquad
\sigma_\mathrm{tot}^2 = \langle d_i^2\rangle = \sigma_\mathrm{coh}^2 + \sigma_\mathrm{inc}^2,
\end{align*}
where:
\begin{itemize}
\item $p_i$: pedestal of the channel.
\item $d_i$: deviation of the channel in the event.
\item $\mathrm{CM}$: common mode of the chip, over its $n$ channels read at the same trigger.
\item $b_i$: slope of the channel against the common mode, least squares over the events.
\item $r_i$: incoherent part.
\item $\langle\,\rangle$: mean over channels and events.
\end{itemize}
Set aside:
\begin{itemize}
\item events with $|\mathrm{CM}| > 6\,s_\mathrm{CM}$: jumps of the chip;
\item entries with $|d_i - \mathrm{CM}| > 5\,s_i$: outliers;
\item $s$: robust width, 1.4826 times the median absolute deviation.
\end{itemize}
Blind channel:
\begin{itemize}
\item its SiPM signal does not reach the chip;
\item takes no common mode: $b_i < 0.75$;
\item excluded from $n$.
\end{itemize}
Units: ADC counts of the high gain unless stated.

Against expectations:
\begin{itemize}
\item Expected (noise model of the simulation): independent channels, no coherent part.
\item Observed: a quarter of the variance is coherent, shared by the 64 channels of a board,
  absent from the blind channels (it enters through the SiPMs) and uncorrelated between
  boards.
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}
\item From 25 to 87.5~ns (Figure~\ref{fig:shaping}, Table~\ref{tab:shaping}):
  $\sigma_\mathrm{coh}$ grows from 6.2 to 9.8; $\sigma_\mathrm{inc}$ falls from 10.6 to 9.6.
\item Low gain: 0.48, 0.46, 0.43 of $\sigma_\mathrm{tot}$ of the high gain at 25, 50, 87.5~ns.
\end{itemize}
\needspace{6\baselineskip}
Against expectations:
\begin{itemize}
\item Expected (white series noise, as in the model): $\sigma \propto t_p^{-1/2}$, that is
  12.3, 8.7, 6.6 at 25, 50, 87.5~ns; the same noise in both gains.
\item Observed: $\sigma_\mathrm{inc}$ falls by 10\% instead of 47\%; $\sigma_\mathrm{coh}$
  grows by 58\%, as white parallel noise ($\propto t_p^{+1/2}$: 6.2, 8.8, 11.6) or a
  low-frequency noise would; the low gain has 0.46 of the noise of the high gain. The noise is
  not series white noise.
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}

\item Observed: the rms falls to 0.88 and 0.69, the pedestal rises by 0.03 and 0.38, by
  board-dependent amounts. A possible cause is a shift of the baseline with the hold delay.
\end{itemize}

\section{Summary of results}

Main findings:
\begin{itemize}
\item The pedestal noise of a channel has a coherent part, shared by the 32 channels of its
  chip, and an incoherent part; at 25~ns the coherent part carries a quarter of the variance.
\item The two chips of a board share their common mode (correlation 0.6); boards are
  independent.
\item The coherent part grows with the shaping time, the incoherent part falls slightly.
\item Both parts narrow together with the window of the peak detector, to 0.59 at 496~ns; the
  pedestal rises with the window, by board-dependent amounts.
\item The six chips of boards 1, 2 and 4 agree within 0.8 counts; no chip jumps.
\item Six channels are blind: two on board 1, four on board 4.
\end{itemize}
