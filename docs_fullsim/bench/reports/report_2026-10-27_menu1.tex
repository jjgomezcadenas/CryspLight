\noindent On 27 October 2026 the noise programme of the Citiroc~1A front end (menu~1) was
repeated on three A5202 boards, 1, 2 and 4, connected to their SiPM matrices through the CAEN
adapter boards and running the upgraded FPGA firmware (0xa7070800). This report gives the
pedestal noise of the three boards, its dependence on the shaping time and on the hold delay,
and the values to be frozen for the noise model of the simulation.

\section{Data}

Nine pedestal runs (Table~\ref{tab:runs}): SiPMs at 36.5~V, below breakdown; periodic trigger
at 1~kHz; preamplifier gains 55 in both paths; peak detectors in peak-stretch mode; low gain
shaped at 87.5~ns. The DAQ reads the boards as 0, 1 and 2; they are boards 1, 2 and 4 of the
bench and are named so throughout.

\section{Method}

For channel $i$ of a chip and event $e$, with $v_i(e)$ the high-gain value:
\begin{align*}
p_i &= \operatorname{median}_e v_i(e), & d_i(e) &= v_i(e) - p_i, &
\mathrm{CM}(e) &= \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i} d_i(e),\\
d_i(e) &= b_i\,\mathrm{CM}(e) + r_i(e), &
\sigma_\mathrm{coh}^2 &= \langle b_i^2\,\mathrm{CM}^2\rangle, &
\sigma_\mathrm{inc}^2 &= \langle r_i^2\rangle, \qquad
\sigma_\mathrm{tot}^2 = \langle d_i^2\rangle = \sigma_\mathrm{coh}^2 + \sigma_\mathrm{inc}^2,
\end{align*}
where $p_i$ is the pedestal, $d_i$ the deviation, $\mathrm{CM}$ the common mode of the chip
over its $n$ channels read at the same trigger, $b_i$ the slope of the channel against the
common mode (a least-squares fit over the events), $r_i$ the incoherent part, and
$\langle\,\rangle$ the mean over channels and events. Events with
$|\mathrm{CM}| > 6\,s_\mathrm{CM}$ (jumps of the chip) and entries with
$|d_i - \mathrm{CM}| > 5\,s_i$ (outliers) are set aside, $s$ being the robust width,
1.4826 times the median absolute deviation. A \emph{blind channel} is one whose SiPM signal
does not reach the chip; it takes no common mode ($b_i < 0.75$) and is excluded from $n$. Noise
values are in ADC counts of the high gain unless stated.

Every channel of the three boards is read; $100 < p_i < 300$ (Figure~\ref{fig:pedestals}).

with a spread of about 1~count over the 64 channels (Figure~\ref{fig:rms}).

common modes of the two chips of a board are correlated at 0.59 to 0.64, those of different
boards at $-0.01$ to 0.02. No chip shows a jump in any run.
