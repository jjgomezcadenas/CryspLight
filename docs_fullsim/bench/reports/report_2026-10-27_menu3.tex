\section{Dishes served}

\begin{itemize}
\item The peak detector is the stage of the Citiroc after the slow shaper. In peak-stretch
  mode it follows the shaped signal from the opening of the window, holds the highest value
  reached, and stops at the hold (HoldDelay after the trigger); that value is what the ADC
  reads.
\item The simulation expresses every charge in photoelectrons (pe); the chip stores ADC
  counts. The gain of a channel, in counts per photoelectron, is the unit of the whole chain.
\item Above breakdown every channel produces dark pulses, several MHz, almost all of one
  photoelectron; with crosstalk, 2, 3, \ldots~pe. With a periodic trigger the window opens at
  a random time: it holds the pedestal, or the dark pulse that fell inside. The spectrum of
  the channel is a ladder of equally spaced peaks at 0, 1, 2, \ldots~pe; the spacing is the
  gain.
\item Menu 3 measures, for every channel of every board:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item the gain at three biases and, at the highest, at three shaping times;
  \item the breakdown voltage, from the gain against the bias;
  \item the pedestal noise of menu~1 in photoelectrons, with the gain just measured.
  \end{itemize}
\item The staircases at two biases give the size of one photoelectron in units of the
  threshold at each.
\end{itemize}

\section{Method}

\subsection{Gain}

What the DAQ does:
\begin{itemize}
\item triggers every millisecond, independently of the SiPMs; the window of 200~ns opens at
  a random time with respect to the dark pulses of each channel;
\item stores, for each event, the high gain of the 64 channels of each board;
\item the result is the spectrum of each channel, $S_i(x)$, over 50\,000 events.
\end{itemize}
What the spectrum shows:
\begin{itemize}
\item a peak at the pedestal (0~pe), from the windows with no dark pulse;
\item peaks at 1, 2, 3, \ldots~pe, from the windows that hold a dark pulse, with or without
  crosstalk; the peaks are equally spaced by the gain;
\item each peak is wider than the previous one, since each avalanche adds its own fluctuation;
\item at the longest shaping the dark pulses pile up and the peaks merge.
\end{itemize}
Definitions:
\begin{equation*}
f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{5} A_n \exp\left[-\frac{(x - p - n g)^2}{2(\sigma_0^2 + n\sigma_1^2)}\right],
\qquad \text{resolved if } \sqrt{\sigma_0^2 + \sigma_1^2} < g/2,
\end{equation*}
\begin{itemize}
\item $f$: the comb of six Gaussians fitted to the spectrum of each channel; $A_n$ the
  population of the $n$-pe peak;
\item $p$: the position of the 0-pe peak (the pedestal); $g$: the gain, in ADC counts per
  photoelectron;
\item $\sigma_0$: the width of the 0-pe peak; $\sigma_1$: the extra width per photoelectron;
\item resolved: the 1-pe peak is narrower than half the spacing, so that neighbouring peaks
  separate; only resolved channels enter the medians;
\item seed of $g$: the spacing of the peaks found on the spectrum at 41~V, where the ladder is
  cleanest; at each higher bias, the gain of the previous one scaled by the overvoltage;
\item left out: the six blind channels (61 and 63 of board~1; 3, 59, 61 and 63 of board~4).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Breakdown voltage}

\begin{equation*}
g = a\,(V - V_\mathrm{bd}),
\end{equation*}
\begin{itemize}
\item $V$: the bias at the SiPM; $V_\mathrm{bd}$: the breakdown voltage of the channel, the
  bias at which its gain vanishes; $a$: the gain per volt of overvoltage;
\item the line is fitted through the gain of the channel at 41, 42 and 43~V, for the channels
  resolved at the three biases.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Noise in photoelectrons}

\begin{equation*}
\sigma^\mathrm{pe} = \sigma / g,
\end{equation*}
\begin{itemize}
\item $\sigma$: the total, coherent or incoherent pedestal noise of menu~1 at HoldDelay 0
  (noise note~\cite{noisenote}), in ADC counts;
\item $g$: the median gain of the board at 43~V and the same shaping.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Staircases}

\begin{itemize}
\item As in menu~2: the edge of the noise $\theta^\mathrm{e}$ and the photoelectron spacing
  $\Delta\theta$ of each channel, from the steps of its trigger rate against the threshold;
\item one DAC unit of the threshold in ADC counts of the high gain: $g / \Delta\theta$.
\end{itemize}

Figure~\ref{fig:breakdown} and Table~\ref{tab:breakdown}:
\begin{itemize}
\item the gain is linear in the bias: $a$ = 41--42 counts per photoelectron per volt;
\item $V_\mathrm{bd}$ = 38.8, 38.8, 39.0~V (medians of boards 1, 2, 4), rms 0.25--0.35~V

\needspace{6\baselineskip}
Against expectations:
\begin{itemize}
\item Expected: the gain of a SiPM is proportional to the overvoltage; the bench set 43~V as
  5~V of overvoltage, that is a breakdown of 38~V.
\item Observed: linear, as expected; the breakdown is 38.8--39.0~V, so the three biases are
  4.1, 3.1 and 2.1~V of overvoltage.
\end{itemize}

\section{Summary of results}

Main findings:
\begin{itemize}
\item At 25~ns the ladder of dark pulses is resolved in nearly every channel (183, 184 and
  161 of 186 at 41, 42 and 43~V); at 50~ns in 110 channels; at 87.5~ns in 26, where the dark
  pulses pile up, as anticipated.
\item The gain is linear in the bias, 41--42 counts per photoelectron per volt. The
  breakdown voltage is 38.8--39.0~V on the three boards, uniform within 0.3~V across the
  channels; the bias of 43~V, set as 5~V of overvoltage, is 4.1~V above breakdown.
\item The gain varies by 2.4--5.6\% across the channels of a board and little with the
  shaping (165--182 counts per photoelectron at 43~V).
\item The pedestal noise of the chain at 43~V is 0.07 photoelectrons per channel at the three
  shapings, half of it coherent at 25~ns; the model of the simulation has 0.11 at 25~ns and
  0.06 at 87.5~ns.
\item One DAC unit of the threshold is 7.8 counts of the high gain at 43~V; one photoelectron
  is 22 units at 43~V and 12.5 at 41~V.
\item Open: at 43~V a fraction of the entries, growing with the shaping from 0.5 to 12\%,
  reads 0 (finding F12).
\end{itemize}
