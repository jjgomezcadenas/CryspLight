\section{Scope}

Build the full simulation specified in \code{fullsim\_design.tex} and compare
it with the bench measurements: BGO and \csitl at ambient temperature with
the Citiroc~1A and with the DT2745 digitizer (data taking the week of
20 October 2026), BGO at 196~K with the digitizer afterwards, Radioroc2 when
the board arrives. This document tracks what is
planned, in progress and done, the decisions taken and the questions open. It
is edited at every step; the physics stays in the design document.

\section{Principles}

\begin{itemize}
\item The pedagogical simulation is the git tag \code{pedsim} and the branch
  \code{main}; the full simulation grows on the branch \code{fullsim}.
  Nothing is replaced: every feature is gated by a configuration key and the
  frozen-output test proves the pedagogical path unchanged.
\item Repository layout for the full simulation: \code{docs\_fullsim/}
  (documents), \code{fullsim\_runs/<campaign>/} (simulation and analysis
  configurations plus the run log of each measurement campaign),
  \code{fullsim\_analysis/} (Python comparison layer, committed figures under
  \code{results/<campaign>/}), engine additions in \code{src/}, the
  electronics emulation in \code{src/frontend/}, tests in \code{test/}.
  Simulation output in the gitignored \code{output/<tag>/}. Measured data
  outside the repository, referenced by path from the campaign configuration.
\item Documents are written before the code they describe; results are
  produced by tracked scripts and committed with their images; every reported
  result states where it lives.
\end{itemize}

\section{Decisions}

\begin{description}
\item[16 Oct 2026] Full simulation as a subproject of CryspLight, not a client
  package: most of the work is engine work under the parity test; one history,
  one test suite.
\item[16 Oct 2026] Additive only. Tag \code{pedsim}; frozen-output test; gated
  features; separate files for the photoelectron records and the emulated
  events; pedagogical configurations and outputs untouched.
\item[16 Oct 2026] Three-stage pipeline with files between stages, so that the
  front end can be re-tuned against data without rerunning the optics.
\item[16 Oct 2026] Grease ($n = 1.42$) in every configuration; not a parameter.
\item[16 Oct 2026] Cold BGO decay time 2.7~\us from the Antarctica runs, in a
  new material file; the Ding file stays for the pedagogical runs.
\item[16 Oct 2026] The Antarctica runs are not simulated (dead channels, noisy
  chain); the first comparison is the Citiroc ambient campaign.
\item[16 Oct 2026] Overvoltage set from the measured breakdown, 3.0~V for BGO
  and 2.7~V for \csitl; recipe in \code{BGOTSCAN/docs/overvoltage\_recipe.tex}.
\item[16 Oct 2026] Measured and emulated events share one HDF5 layout; the
  bench files are converted to it as the first analysis step.
\item[16 Oct 2026] No waveform-digitiser emulation. \emph{Reversed the
  same day}, see below.
\item[16 Oct 2026] The DT2745 digitizer is a third front end of the
  simulation, emulated on the avalanche train (gated charge with baseline
  subtraction, integrated noise, sampled-amplitude trigger) rather than
  through waveforms. Reason: the Citiroc at 87.5~ns stores 0.07 of the
  photoelectrons of cold BGO, so the cold runs are taken with the digitizer,
  and the ambient runs with both readouts give a parameter-free test of the
  stored fraction. The waveform processing of the measured data (baseline,
  single-photoelectron search) stays on the data side.
\item[16 Oct 2026] Digitizer noise: nominal (4 counts rms, white,
  independent channels) until the new runs; the low-frequency and
  group-correlated terms are added then as a measured input.
\item[16 Oct 2026] Afterpulsing 1\% at 3~V, scaling with the square of the
  overvoltage, 100~ns; not resolvable with the Citiroc.
\item[16 Oct 2026] Intrinsic resolution 5\% FWHM at 511~keV for both
  crystals; the non-proportionality curve of BGO (Gentile et al.) as a
  later refinement; Moszynski et al.\ to be checked.
\item[16 Oct 2026] Ambient digitizer runs at gain 1 and 43~V (5~V of
  overvoltage), the simplest setting for the bench; record 10~\us with
  1~\us pre-trigger. Threshold 60~counts, majority 2, \emph{corrected on
  17 Oct} by the emulated scan to 120~counts with majority 2 (or 160
  single channel); Citiroc at 3~V: 8~photoelectron-equivalents single
  channel (or 5 with majority 2). Recipe and configurations updated.
\end{description}

\section{Open questions}

\begin{itemize}
\item Reflectivity of the tile surface between channels (default diffuse 0.5;
  to fit on the occupancy maps).
\item BGO light yield at ambient and at 196~K for this crystal; BGO intrinsic
  resolution.
\item Citiroc settings of the ambient runs (shaping time, gains, hold delay,
  thresholds, trigger definition in the FPGA) and the ADC; to be written in
  the run log.
\item Angular dependence of the PDE through the resin from grease.
\item Citiroc shaper transfer function and noise spectrum; whether a
  single-photoelectron waveform can be probed on the test board.
\item Temperature of the SiPMs in the cold run (resolved by the measured
  breakdown; a thermometer on the SiPM board would still help).
\item Whether the digitizer gain stage lowers the noise per photoelectron
  (baseline runs at gains 1, 2, 4, 8 with the SiPMs unbiased); decides the
  overvoltage of the cold runs.
\item Whether the Radioroc2 at 1.8~\us shaping (0.55 of the photoelectrons
  of cold BGO) is usable in the cold, or a less cold point is preferable.
\item The format of the WaveDump2 output files and the event-building of the
  64 channels, for the converter.
\end{itemize}

\section{Log}

\begin{description}
\item[16 Oct 2026] Project assessed; datasheets read (Citiroc 1A, Radioroc2,
  S14160/S14161); Antarctica draft read; tag and branch; design document
  v0.1 and this plan; overvoltage recipe written.
\item[16 Oct 2026] Design document reviewed with JJ: measured data (one
  high-gain charge and the hit register), afterpulsing, the stored fraction
  recomputed with the avalanche pulse (0.39/0.15/0.07), digitizer manual
  (UM8717) read and the DT2745 added as a third front end; digitizer section
  added to the overvoltage recipe; trigger thresholds for the Citiroc and
  the digitizer estimated for the ambient runs.
\item[17 Oct 2026] Task 0.4: frozen-output test added and passing; the
  digests do not depend on the thread count and change with the seed or
  with any parameter.
\item[17 Oct 2026] The optical stage records every photon that reaches an
  active area (\code{pde = 1} in the kernel) and the SiPM stage applies the
  detection efficiency, which depends on the overvoltage and the wavelength,
  by thinning the records. Thinning a Poisson process is exactly equivalent
  to applying the efficiency photon by photon, and the overvoltage scan of
  task 0.13 then reuses one optical run per configuration.
\item[17 Oct 2026] First optical run of the ambient BGO configuration on the
  GPU (400\,000 decays, 35~min while the test suite was competing for the
  machine; 5000 decays take 3.7~s alone): 20.8\% of the decays deposit
  energy, 13.0\% give a 511~keV photopeak event; at 511~keV 3883 photons
  emitted, 3253 reach the active areas (collection 0.838), FWHM 4.1\% from
  photon statistics alone; brightest channel median 112 photons, 1\% above
  451; mean map flat within 10\% with the edge channels 10\% lower. The
  acceptance of the 511~keV pair is twice that of one gamma (either partner),
  corrected in the design document.
\item[17 Oct 2026] First optical run of the ambient \csitl configuration
  (100\,000 decays, 577~s on the GPU): 15.9\% of the decays deposit energy,
  7.1\% give a 511~keV photopeak event; 27\,600 photons emitted at 511~keV,
  23\,200 reach the active areas (collection 0.841); brightest channel
  median 541 photons, 1\% above 4050, which at 2.7~V (PDE 0.38 at 550~nm)
  is 1540 photoelectrons, above the Citiroc ceiling of 1000.
\item[17 Oct 2026] The first front-end emulations showed that at ambient
  temperature the dark pulses with crosstalk companions set the trigger
  threshold, not the single dark photoelectron: a dark avalanche with four
  or more companions occurs about once per 2~\us window over the 64
  channels at 3~V (crosstalk 9\%) and twice at 5~V (crosstalk 19\%), so a
  single-channel threshold of 4 to 6 photoelectrons, or 60 counts on the
  digitizer at 43~V, triggers on dark pulses in most windows. The threshold
  scan of \code{fullsim\_analysis/trigger\_scan.jl} replaces the
  hand estimates; the recipe for the bench must be updated accordingly.
\item[17 Oct 2026] Task 0.5: tiled readout layout. The S14161-6050HS-04 tile
  is 25.0~mm (datasheet outline), so the matrix is 50.0~mm and overhangs the
  crystal by 1.0~mm per side, not 49.6 and 0.8 as first written; the active
  fraction of the footprint, 93.4\%, was right. Design document corrected.
\end{description}
